ABOUT THIS SET
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -

The signs symbols within this brush set were extracted from Eugene Henry Fricx’s Cartes des Paysbas et des Frontieres de France—an incredibly detailed 18th Century map of northern France and Belgium. It was created over a period of twenty-one years and fully published in 1727. Jacobus Harrewijn (anglicized "Harrewyn") etched the set and so he gets the honor of the name, and I appreciate his choice to emphasize towns and villages, cities and manor houses, farms and windmills, gallows and chapels. It’s a map focused on the developed over the natural. Quite different from other maps from the same era.

More details on this set can be found at: blog.kmalexander.com

Thanks for download this brush set and I hope you find them useful.
